## Your call (not applied)

### 1. Menus cluster: Radix `modal` vs MRT `disableScrollLock` (5 menus)
MRT sets `disableScrollLock` on every Menu (ColumnAction:330, FilterOption:251, RowAction:77, CellAction:94, ShowHideColumns:106; Popover.js:118 default false). Radix DropdownMenu `modal = true` (react-dropdown-menu dist:29) wraps Content in RemoveScroll (react-menu dist:139, :197), so SRT locks page scroll; the SRT Notes saying Radix "does not scroll-lock" are wrong.
- A: keep `modal` default. Differs from MRT on one axis (page scroll locked while a menu is open). Focus trap and outside-click swallowing match MUI Modal.
- B: `modal={false}` on all five roots. Page scrolls like MRT, but the fixed ghost span is frozen at the open-time rect so the menu stops following its anchor (MUI Popover repositions on window scroll, Popover.js:254), the focus trap is dropped, and an outside click lands on the element under the pointer (MUI backdrop swallows it).
Recommendation: A. Fix the Note wording in the same commit as each menu's focus-return fix (comment rides along with a code change, not standalone).

### 2. Menus cluster: dense selector (5 menus)
MUI dense on MenuItem = minHeight 32, paddingTop/Bottom 4, body2 (MenuItem.js:136-141). MRT's own items pin `py: '6px'` by sx (MRT_ActionMenuItem.tsx:36, MRT_ShowHideColumnsMenuItems.tsx:132), so in MRT compact density changes no padding on any MRT-rendered item; only user-supplied plain MenuItems tighten, and body2 = shadcn `text-sm` already. SRT's `[&>*]:py-1` (FilterOption:24, RowAction:26, CellAction:19) also hits DropdownMenuSeparator (`h-px` + `py-1` under border-box = 8px bar); ColumnAction:21 and ShowHideColumns:24 use `[&_[role=menuitem]]:py-1`, which shrinks SRT's own items MRT never shrinks.
- A: delete the dense cva in all five menus; keep `// MenuListProps dense` commented at MRT's position with a one-line Note. Loss: user-rendered DropdownMenuItems stay 6px in compact (MUI 4px).
- B: unify on `[&_[role=menuitem]]:py-1`. Loss: every SRT item drops to 4px in compact (MRT 6px).
Recommendation: A (matches MRT's rendered result for every MRT-owned item; removes the separator defect).
